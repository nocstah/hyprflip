## Reddit draft

**Title:** Hyprflip: Gmail on the front, WhatsApp + Telegram on the back

I've been building a Hyprland plugin that gives your windows a back side.
This is my actual workspace: Gmail on the front, with WhatsApp and Telegram
sharing the back.

Flip between the two sides, or temporarily unfold the card to use all three
apps together. They stay live, and folding brings them back into the same space.

Cards hold up to five apps per side on Omarchy's default dwindle layout, with
saved cards, pane reordering and replacing an app without rebuilding the card.
It currently targets Hyprland 0.56.2; the core plugin is all you need.

Source and instructions: https://github.com/nocstah/hyprflip

I'd like feedback on where this would help your workflow, and where ordinary
tabs or workspaces would still be easier.
